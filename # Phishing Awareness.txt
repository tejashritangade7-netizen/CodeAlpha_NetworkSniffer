# Phishing Awareness Training Guide - CodeAlpha

1. What is Phishing?
Phishing is a social engineering attack where attackers impersonate trustworthy entities to steal sensitive data like passwords and financial details.

2. Common Types of Phishing
- Email Phishing: Deceptive emails claiming urgent action.
- Spear Phishing: Targeted attacks aimed at specific individuals.
- Smishing & Vishing: Phishing via SMS or voice calls.

3. How to Spot Phishing Indicators
- Mismatched sender domain names.
- Generic greetings (e.g., "Dear Customer").
- Urgent demand for personal or login info.
- Suspicious hyperlinks or unknown attachments.

4. Best Practices & Prevention Tips
- Always verify the URL before clicking.
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
- Never share OTPs, passwords, or personal credentials.
